# G7 Government Spending Anki Deck — Design Spec

## Overview

Direct-to-Anki deck for drilling government spending composition across G7 nations using the Enhanced Cloze Type-In note type. Two sub-decks:

- **Government Spending::By Nation** — Per-country budget breakdown (% of total spending) for each G7 nation, plus a US-specific agency view. 32 cards.
- **Government Spending::By Category** — Cross-country comparison by COFOG division (% of GDP). 40 cards.

**72 cards total.** Each card presents ranked items as typed clozes with an SVG pie/bar chart on the answer side.

**Goal**: Build intuition for the structure and evolution of government spending across major economies — useful for forecasting and comparative socio-economic reasoning.

**Not part of the knowledge-base TUI pipeline** — standalone script suite producing `.apkg` files for direct Anki import.

## Countries

G7 nations: **USA, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Canada, Japan**

## Data Sources

### OECD COFOG (primary)

**Classification of Functions of Government** — standardized functional spending taxonomy across all OECD members. COFOG Level 1 has 10 divisions:

1. General Public Services
2. Defence
3. Public Order & Safety
4. Economic Affairs
5. Environmental Protection
6. Housing & Community Amenities
7. Health
8. Recreation, Culture & Religion
9. Education
10. Social Protection

Available via the OECD data API or bulk download. Provides both % of GDP and % of total spending for each division × country × year.

### OMB Historical Tables (US agency view only)

- **Table 4.1** — Outlays by Agency (organizational view)

Published as Excel (.xlsx) on whitehouse.gov/omb. Used only for the US agency breakdown cards in the By Nation deck.

### Fiscal Year Snapshots

| Year | Rationale |
|------|-----------|
| 2015 | Pre-Trump baseline, normal peacetime budget |
| 2020 | COVID-era spike in spending |
| 2023 | Post-COVID normalization, Ukraine war ongoing |
| 2025 | Most recent / current |

Use actual (not estimated) figures where available. If 2025 actuals are not yet published for a given source, use the most recent available year and note it in card tags. OECD COFOG data can lag 1-2 years; if a target year is unavailable, use the nearest available year.

If a country does not report a COFOG division, omit it from that card (card may have fewer than 10 items for By Nation, or fewer than 7 for By Category).

## Deck Structure

### Government Spending::By Nation

Per-country cards showing the top 10 COFOG divisions ranked by **% of total government spending**. All 10 divisions are included (no "Other" needed since COFOG L1 is exhaustive at 10 divisions).

**COFOG cards**: 7 countries × 4 years = **28 cards**

Example card:
```
2023 Japan Government Spending by Category (% of total):<br>
1. {{c1::Social Protection - 37%}}<br>
2. {{c1::Health - 20%}}<br>
3. {{c1::General Public Services - 14%}}<br>
...
10. {{c1::Environmental Protection - 1%}}
```

**US agency cards**: 4 years × 1 = **4 cards** (from OMB Table 4.1)

Top 10 agencies by dollar amount, shown as % of total outlays. Remaining agencies lumped into "Other" (on pie chart only, not as a typed cloze).

Example card:
```
FY2023 US Federal Spending by Agency (% of total):<br>
1. {{c1::Social Security Administration - 20%}}<br>
2. {{c1::Dept. of Health & Human Services - 18%}}<br>
...
10. {{c1::Dept. of Homeland Security - 2%}}
```

**By Nation total: 32 cards**

Tags: `govt_spending`, `by_nation`, `{country}`, `{year}`, and `by_agency` for the US agency cards.

### Government Spending::By Category

Cross-country comparison cards showing all 7 G7 nations ranked by **% of GDP** spent on each COFOG division.

10 COFOG divisions × 4 years = **40 cards**

Example card:
```
2023 G7 Defence Spending (% of GDP):<br>
1. {{c1::USA - 3.4%}}<br>
2. {{c1::UK - 2.3%}}<br>
3. {{c1::France - 1.9%}}<br>
4. {{c1::Italy - 1.5%}}<br>
5. {{c1::Canada - 1.3%}}<br>
6. {{c1::Germany - 1.3%}}<br>
7. {{c1::Japan - 1.1%}}
```

7 items per card (one per G7 nation), ranked by value descending.

Tags: `govt_spending`, `by_category`, `{cofog_division}`, `{year}`

**By Category total: 40 cards**

## Card Format

Uses the **Enhanced Cloze Type-In 1.0** note type. All line items use `{{c1::...}}` so they are all clozed simultaneously — user types each answer in sequence using Tab to advance.

Percentages rounded to nearest integer for By Nation cards (% of total). One decimal place for By Category cards (% of GDP) since values are smaller and closer together.

### SVG Visualization

Generated by matplotlib, embedded as inline SVG in the `Note` field:

**By Nation cards (pie chart)**:
- Pie chart with slices for each COFOG division (or agency), labeled with name + percentage
- "Other" slice in grey for US agency cards
- Categorical colorblind-friendly palette (e.g., `tab10`)
- ~400px wide, readable on desktop and mobile

**By Category cards (horizontal bar chart)**:
- Horizontal bar chart with one bar per G7 nation
- Bars labeled with country + percentage
- Sorted to match the card's ranking order
- More natural than a pie chart for cross-country comparison

No title on charts (card header provides context). The back side template auto-reveals the Note field, so the chart is visible on flip.

### Anki Fields

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| `Content` | Cloze-formatted text |
| `Note` | SVG chart (pie or bar) |
| `Mnemonics` | (empty) |
| `Extra` | (empty) |
| `Cloze99` | (empty, required by note type) |

## Pipeline Architecture

Two-stage pipeline with a CSV inspection seam:

```
OECD COFOG data ──┐
                   ├──→ [parse] ──→ parsed CSVs ──→ [build_cards] ──→ cards.csv ──→ [export] ──→ .apkg
OMB Excel files ──┘
```

### Stage 1: Data Acquisition & Parsing

**`scripts/govt_spending/fetch_data.py`** — Downloads:
- OECD COFOG data for G7 nations (API or bulk download) → `data/govt_spending/raw/`
- OMB Table 4.1 Excel file → `data/govt_spending/raw/`

**`scripts/govt_spending/parse_cofog.py`** — Parses OECD data, extracts the 4 target years for all 7 G7 nations × 10 COFOG divisions. Outputs:
- `data/govt_spending/cofog_by_nation.csv` — % of total spending, per country per year
- `data/govt_spending/cofog_by_category.csv` — % of GDP, per division per year

Columns for both: `year`, `country`, `cofog_division`, `pct_of_total` or `pct_of_gdp`, `amount_billions_usd`

**`scripts/govt_spending/parse_omb_agency.py`** — Parses OMB Table 4.1 for US agency data. Extracts top 10 agencies at the top-level (skip subtotals). Outputs:
- `data/govt_spending/us_agency.csv`

Columns: `fiscal_year`, `rank`, `agency`, `amount_billions`, `pct_of_total`

### Stage 2: Card Building & Export

**`scripts/govt_spending/build_cards.py`** — Reads parsed CSVs, generates cloze text and SVG charts, outputs `data/govt_spending/cards.csv` with columns:
- `card_id` — e.g., `by_nation_japan_2023`, `by_category_defence_2023`, `by_agency_usa_2023`
- `deck` — `by_nation` or `by_category`
- `content` — Full cloze-formatted text
- `note` — SVG chart as inline string
- `tags` — Space-separated tag list

This CSV is the **inspection/edit seam** — review and hand-edit before exporting.

**`scripts/govt_spending/export_apkg.py`** — Reads `cards.csv` and builds the `.apkg` using `genanki`:
- Model mirrors Enhanced Cloze Type-In 1.0 (same fields). Model ID must match the existing note type in the Anki collection so imported cards use the installed templates — extract from the addon's `model.py` or from an existing export.
- Two decks: `Government Spending::By Nation` and `Government Spending::By Category`
- Stable GUIDs derived from `card_id` for safe re-import (updates existing cards, no duplicates)

## Re-run Workflow

When new data is available:
1. Re-run `fetch_data.py` to download updated source files
2. Add new years to the target list in the parse scripts
3. Re-run parse → build_cards → inspect `cards.csv` → export_apkg
4. Import `.apkg` into Anki — existing cards update, new cards are added
